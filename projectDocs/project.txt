Smart India Hackathon 2025

Problem Title
ERP-based Integrated Student Management System

Problem Description

In many colleges, student-related processes such as admissions, fee collection, hostel allocation, and examination records are managed through separate ledgers and manual entries. This fragmented approach creates multiple inefficiencies: students must queue at different counters for different services, staff re-enter identical data across departments, and administrators lack a real-time, consolidated institutional overview.
While comprehensive ERP systems exist to solve these issues, they are often financially out of reach for public colleges. However, much of the required functionality—customized online forms, central data tables, automated receipts, and real-time dashboards—already exists within widely available cloud office suites. By intelligently connecting these services, institutions can establish a single source of truth without heavy capital investments.

An ideal solution should provide:

Streamlined admission intake with integrated forms feeding directly into a centralized student database.
Automated fee collection with instant digital receipts and ledger updates.
Live hostel and library occupancy tracking connected to the same core database.
Real-time dashboards presenting summary reports and key performance metrics to higher officials.
Security and governance features like role-based access control, automated backups, and encrypted storage.
Because staff members are already familiar with basic spreadsheet and form tools, such a system would have a shallow learning curve, ensuring quick adoption and reducing training overhead.


Expected Solution
Hackathon participants are expected to demonstrate how lightweight scripting, thoughtful process mapping, and interlinking of familiar cloud-based services can create a low-cost ERP alternative.
The solution should:
Show how admission data flows seamlessly into a centralized student database.
Enable financial transactions that automatically generate digital receipts.
Update hostel and library records in real time with minimal manual intervention.
Provide role-based dashboards offering administrators a real-time institutional overview.
Ensure data security, backup, and access control from the ground up.
The goal is to prove that any college can replicate this system using affordable, widely available tools, thus empowering institutions with efficient student management without large-scale ERP investments.
